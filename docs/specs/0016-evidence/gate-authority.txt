The page says nothing, in the text read, about how long a job waits for approval before it fails: the 43,200 minutes (30 days) above is the largest *wait timer*, a delay, not an approval deadline. The spec therefore does not rely on any GitHub deadline: the workflow enforces its own expiry (see the spec, Revisions 2026-10-30 round 1).
